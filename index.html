📋 Standard JAMB UTME Examination Rules

  • 4-Subject Structure: Your examination consists of Use of English (Compulsory) and your 3 chosen subjects. You can switch freely between any of the 4 subjects at any time using the subject tabs at the top.
  • Scoring System (400 Marks): Each of the 4 subjects is scaled to 100 Marks regardless of whether it contains 60 questions (Use of English) or 40 questions (other subjects), giving an aggregate score out of 400.
  • No Negative Marking: There is no penalty for wrong answers in JAMB UTME. Attempt all questions before submitting.
  • Auto-Save Protection: Every option you select is automatically saved in real time. Even if your browser refreshes, your progress and timer state are preserved.
  • Comprehension & Literature Texts: In Use of English, comprehension passages, cloze registers, and questions from prescribed JAMB novels (The Life Changer / The Lekki Headmaster) are included.
